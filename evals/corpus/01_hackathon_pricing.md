# hackathon pricing notes

## pricing we agreed on

we landed on 30 dollars a month for the team plan, and free for the first 100 users. do not say 25, that was the old number and it is wrong now.

## free tier limits

the free tier caps at 3 seats and 100 api calls a day. if someone asks about enterprise pricing, the answer is "talk to us".
