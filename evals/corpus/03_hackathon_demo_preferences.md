# demo preferences

how the owner wants the demo run.

## demo flow preferences

the owner prefers running the demo live rather than slides. always ask for screen share first. keep the live demo under 4 minutes or she gets nervous.

## pitch voice

during pitches she likes starting with the problem, not the solution. no buzzwords, she hates "synergy".
