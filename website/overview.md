# prixy

prixy is a personal presence proxy for people who need something from a meeting, event, or digital session without needing to be there themselves.

the product starts with a direct instruction. a person tells prixy where to go and what information to bring back. prixy handles the presence, follows the session, asks approved questions, and returns the requested answer. the full source material can remain available without becoming mandatory reading.

prixy is for people with overloaded calendars, inconvenient timezones, conflicting obligations, limited attention, or a simple preference not to spend an hour acquiring one fact.

using prixy should feel quiet, direct, and personal. it does not create work to prove it saved work. it stays out of the way until it has something useful to report.
